# ClinicCare-Lite — Patient User Guide

This guide explains how to use ClinicCare-Lite as a patient. It covers
creating an account, viewing your health tasks, submitting files,
tracking your own progress, and managing your account settings.

Important: ClinicCare-Lite is an administrative and communication
tool. It does not diagnose you, interpret your health data, or replace
medical advice. Always follow the guidance given to you directly by
your clinician.

---

1. Creating an account

1. Go to the ClinicCare-Lite login page and click **"Don't have an
   account? Register"**.
2. Choose **Patient** as your role.
3. Enter your **User ID**. This must be exactly 8 digits, ending in
   the year you're registering (for example, `12342026` if you're
   registering in 2026). Valid years are 2022 through 2028.
4. Enter your name and email address.
5. Choose a password that includes:
   - At least 8 characters
   - At least one uppercase letter
   - At least one lowercase letter
   - At least one number
   - At least one special character (`!@#$%^&*`)
6. Click **Register**. If everything is valid, you'll be sent back to
   the login page to sign in.

## 2. Logging in and out

- Enter your User ID and password on the login page, then click
  **Log in**.
- To log out at any time, click the **Log out** button in the top
  right of your dashboard.

## 3. Understanding your dashboard

When you log in, you'll see your personal dashboard with several
sections:

### Summary cards
At the top, three cards show:
- **Overdue** — tasks past their due date that you haven't submitted
- **Pending** — tasks waiting for you to submit
- **Total tasks** — everything ever assigned to you

### Your activity summary
This section is **private and visible only to you** — no other
patient can see it, and it is never used to compare you against
anyone else. It shows:
- **Tasks submitted** — the percentage of your assigned tasks you've
  submitted a file for
- **Submitted on time** — the percentage submitted by their due date
- **Current on-time streak** — how many of your most recent tasks in
  a row were submitted on time
- **Completed (last 30 days)** — how many tasks you've completed
  recently
- **Engagement points** — a simple point total: you earn points for
  submitting tasks on time and for attending scheduled appointments
- **On-time appointment attendance** — the percentage of your past
  appointments you attended, once any have been marked

This section only tracks whether you submitted things and attended
appointments on time — it never looks at or judges the actual content
of what you submit.

### Your health tasks
Each task your clinic has assigned you appears here with:
- A title and description of what's being asked of you
- The due date
- A status badge: **Pending**, **Overdue**, **Reviewed - Normal**,
  **Needs Follow-up**, or **Escalated**
- Once you've submitted a file, the submission date/time and a link
  to download your own file
- Any notes your clinician has added after reviewing your submission

### Upcoming appointments
Shows your scheduled appointments, soonest first, with the date, time,
and a short reason label.

### Messages
Shows messages between you and your clinic. **This is for non-urgent
questions only and is not monitored continuously — in an emergency,
contact your local emergency services immediately.**

## 4. Submitting a file for a task

1. Find the task on your dashboard.
2. Click **Choose File** and select the file from your device.
3. Only these file types are accepted:
   - `.txt`
   - `.csv`
   - `.pdf`
4. Files must be under 5 MB.
5. Click **Upload**.

If your file is accepted, you'll see a confirmation message. If the
task expects specific information (for example, a date and a reading
in a CSV file), the system will also check that:
- All expected columns are present
- No required fields are left empty
- Dates and numbers are in a valid format

If something's missing or formatted incorrectly, you'll see a plain
message explaining exactly what to fix — for example, *"The date
field is missing"* or *"Row 2 is missing a value for 'reading'"*.
These messages only ever describe the structure of your file. They
never comment on what your data means medically.

## 5. Resubmitting a file

- If your task status is **Pending** or **Needs Follow-up**, you can
  submit a new file at any time — your previous file is kept safely,
  not deleted, in case it's needed later.
- Once a clinician has marked your submission **Reviewed - Normal** or
  **Escalated**, that task is considered complete and can no longer
  be resubmitted.

## 6. Downloading your own files

Next to any submission you've made, click **Download my file** to get
a copy of exactly what you uploaded. You can only ever download your
own files — not another patient's.

## 7. Switching between light and dark themes

Click **Switch to Dark** (or **Switch to Colourful**) in the top right
of your dashboard at any time. Your choice is saved automatically and
will still be applied the next time you log in.

## 8. Keeping your information private

- Your engagement summary, submissions, and messages are visible only
  to you and your clinic — never to other patients.
- Always log out when using a shared or public computer.
- Never share your password with anyone, including clinic staff —
  they will never need it.

## 9. Getting help

If something isn't working as expected, or a task's instructions are
unclear, use the **Messages** section to contact your clinic directly
for non-urgent questions. For anything urgent or medical, contact
your clinic by phone or visit in person — do not rely on this app for
emergencies.
